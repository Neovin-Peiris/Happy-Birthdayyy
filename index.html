My love,

I don't really know how to write this without sounding like a lovesick mess, so I'm just going to let myself sound like one. Since December you've had this hold on me I genuinely didn't see coming — the kind where my chest still does something stupid every time your name lights up my phone, months in. I don't think that's supposed to happen this fast. It did anyway.

You run yourself ragged for other people all day and all night — long shifts, no sleep, patients who need you more than you'll ever admit you need anyone — and somehow you still come home to me with something left over. That undoes me a little, every single time. You give so much of yourself away, and I get to keep some of it. I don't know what I did to deserve that.

I love your smile so much it's honestly embarrassing. I love your eyes and the way they pull me in before you've even said anything. I love the sound of your voice when you're talking to me — low and soft one second, teasing the next — and what it does to me every single time. I love that you bite me, just to be a menace, just because you can, and I love it way more than I should admit out loud. And your laugh — the real, loud, unfiltered one — is my favorite sound in the entire world. I chase it on purpose.

There's so much more I feel for you that I don't even have the words for. It just sits warm in my chest, all the time, whenever I think about you. Which is often. Which is basically always.

Happy birthday, my love. If you're reading this on shift, tucked into some tiny break room — breathe, drink some water, and know that somewhere out here my whole heart is yours. I am so unbelievably in love with you. I'll be counting down until you're back in my arms.

Yours, hopelessly and completely.
